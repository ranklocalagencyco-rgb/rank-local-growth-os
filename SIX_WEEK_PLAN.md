# Six-Week Plan

## Week 1
Foundation: app, database, auth, campaigns/prospects foundations, jobs.

## Week 2
Discovery + deterministic website auditing.

## Week 3
Opportunity scoring + Qwen/OpenRouter intelligence.

## Week 4
Grounded outreach drafts + approval + Gmail.

## Week 5
Follow-ups + reply handling + suppression + pipeline.

## Week 6
Meeting briefs + analytics + hardening + deployment.

## Scope freeze
Not in the six-week V1:
- full marketing/customer/finance departments
- Hermes as core runtime
- multi-tenant SaaS
- mobile app
- voice agents
- autonomous proposal pricing
- elaborate agent-map UI
